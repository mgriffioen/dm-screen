# Player Screen

Everything in the story worth putting on the **Echo's player screen**, in the order it comes up. Tap **📺 Show** on any box to send it. The same button appears beside these passages on the session and puzzle pages. Tap the Echo to dismiss.

**What goes here:** words the characters *read* (inscriptions, carvings, letters), puzzles the table needs to stare at, and menus the players choose from. Read-aloud boxes stay spoken, so only their last, sharpest line is here.

> **Never send** anything in the ✋ list at the bottom. The player screen is for the whole table.

## Session 3 — Shangri-La

### Title card: Shangri-La
- **When:** The session starts.
> SHANGRI-LA
> Where summer never ends.

### The Hound's Hearth
- **When:** Whenever someone asks about the seventh lodge.
> *"Oh, nobody stays in that one."*

### The Heart Lodge: the Lintel
- **When:** When Dusty's character opens his door (and only then).
> *"I hear it in the deep heart's core."*

### Confiscated from Guests
- **When:** Someone looks through the finery in the watchful-eye lodge.
> Behind the finery, a crate of sky-rockets, stenciled:
> *"Confiscated from guests."*

### The Reveal
- **When:** Movement 2, after you've read the box. Cut the lights, then send.
> You are not in a valley.
> You are in a *mouth.*

### Lair Actions (the Heart)
- **When:** Phase B, at initiative 20. Show Erik first; send after he has had his chance to warn. Send only the one you rolled.

#### 1 · The Feast Hungers
> **The Feast Hungers**
> Tables lash out with tablecloth tongues. DC 13 DEX or restrained.

#### 2 · The Springs Overflow
> **The Springs Overflow**
> A 10-ft area floods with digestive water. 2d6 acid, DC 13 CON for half.

#### 3 · The Song Slows
> **The Song Slows**
> Everyone makes a DC 13 WIS save.

#### 4 · The Chute
> **The Chute**
> The yellow tongue lashes 30 ft from the lakebed. DC 13 DEX or be pulled 20 ft toward the Heart.

#### 5 · The Hill Tilts
> **The Hill Tilts**
> Everyone slides 10 ft toward the Heart.

#### 6 · A Gift
> **A Gift**
> The Keeper holds something out to you. Will you take it?

### The Escape
- **When:** Phase C, as the chase starts.
> The Gate is closing.
> 3 rounds. Each round: say how you get past it, then roll any check you like. DC 12.

### Leaving the Vale
- **When:** At the Gate, as the bill comes due.
> *"Leaving so soon? Well, let's settle what you took."*
>
> Give back each pleasure or gift, or pay 1d6 HP for it.

### The Morning After
- **When:** The epilogue's closing moments.
> What does your character do the next morning?

### The Second Invitation
- **When:** Final stinger, Twist #9 only.
> A smaller card. Cheaper gilt. A tiny spiral, slightly wobbly.
> *"A more intimate retreat. Under new management."*

## ✋ Never send these

These are secret, or spoil a mystery the players should feel out. They stay on folded notes.

- **Erik's notes:** the Seer's visions, his Session Zero card, the Seer's Floor key, his reflection under the Glass Mere, and the looping sunset.
- **Dusty's notes:** his sealed envelope, his reflection in the white robe, his handwriting in the ledger, the Returned recognizing him, and "You remember now."
- **The Contentment track and The Remembering.** Players see their pips, but what they mean is theirs to work out.
- **The Mayor's spiral ring.** It's a Perception clue for the first player who notices.
- **Lodge baits, tells and the Hound's Hearth.** Show the lodge pictures from Maps instead.
